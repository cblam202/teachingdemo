Methodology

The test compares the population means using the observed difference, sample 1 minus sample 2.

t=x¯1−x¯2SEt=\frac{\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2}{SE}

Equal variances

sp2=(n1−1)s12+(n2−1)s22n1+n2−2s_p^2=\frac{(n_1-1)s_1^2+(n_2-1)s_2^2}{n_1+n_2-2}
SE=sp1n1+1n2SE=s_p\sqrt{\frac{1}{n_1}+\frac{1}{n_2}}
df=n1+n2−2df=n_1+n_2-2

Unequal variances

SE=s12n1+s22n2SE=\sqrt{\frac{s_1^2}{n_1}+\frac{s_2^2}{n_2}}

Satterthwaite (1946)

df=(s12n1+s22n2)2(s12/n1)2n1−1+(s22/n2)2n2−1df=\frac{\left(\frac{s_1^2}{n_1}+\frac{s_2^2}{n_2}\right)^2}{\frac{(s_1^2/n_1)^2}{n_1-1}+\frac{(s_2^2/n_2)^2}{n_2-1}}

Welch (1947)

df=−2+(s12n1+s22n2)2(s12/n1)2n1+1+(s22/n2)2n2+1df=-2+\frac{\left(\frac{s_1^2}{n_1}+\frac{s_2^2}{n_2}\right)^2}{\frac{(s_1^2/n_1)^2}{n_1+1}+\frac{(s_2^2/n_2)^2}{n_2+1}}

Dependent (paired) t-test

For each complete pair, Dᵢ = xᵢ − yᵢ. Let D̄ be the mean difference, sD its sample SD, and n the number of complete pairs.

SE=sDnSE=\frac{s_D}{\sqrt{n}}
t=DˉSEt=\frac{\bar D}{SE}
df=n−1df=n-1

Sample SD

Each sample SD uses n − 1 in the denominator. Independent tests omit missing values separately; paired tests omit incomplete pairs.

Confidence interval

Confidence level is 100(1 − α)%. For two-tailed tests, tcrit is the 1 − α/2 quantile and the interval is shown below. For right-tailed tests, tcrit is the 1 − α quantile and the interval extends from the lower bound to ∞. For left-tailed tests, it extends from −∞ to the upper bound.

Independent samples:

[xˉ1−xˉ2−tcrit×SE,  xˉ1−xˉ2+tcrit×SE][\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2-t_{\text{crit}}\times SE,\;\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2+t_{\text{crit}}\times SE]

Paired samples:

[Dˉ−tcrit×SE,  Dˉ+tcrit×SE][\bar D-t_{\text{crit}}\times SE,\;\bar D+t_{\text{crit}}\times SE]

p-value and critical values

The p-value is the area beyond the observed statistic in the selected tail, or both tails for a two-tailed test. Critical values leave α in the selected tail, or α/2 in each tail. Reject H₀ when p-value < α.
